Meta Ads Audience for an EdTech Company's Digital Marketing Course

Project Overview

This project focuses on creating an optimized Meta Ads audience for an EdTech company's Digital Marketing course.

The objective is to reach potential students, fresh graduates, early-career professionals, freelancers, and business owners who are interested in learning digital marketing.

Internship Details

- Internship Domain: Digital Marketing
- Internship Platform: upskill Campus
- Industrial Partner: UniConverge Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (UCT)
- Organization: The IoT Academy
- University: Maharaja Agrasen University
- Project: Meta Ads Audience Optimization

Project Objectives

- Identify the right target audience for a Digital Marketing course.
- Select suitable locations, age groups, languages, interests, and behaviors.
- Create and compare different audience segments.
- Understand campaign performance using marketing metrics.
- Plan budget optimization and audience retargeting.

Tools and Technologies

- Meta Ads Manager
- Facebook and Instagram Ads
- Meta Business Help Center
- Meta Blueprint
- Meta Pixel and Conversion Events

Target Audience

The project focuses on three audience segments:

1. College Students and Recent Graduates
2. Early-Career Professionals
3. Freelancers and Business Owners

The proposed audience includes people aged 18–35, with targeting based on relevant locations, interests, demographics, and online behaviors.

Campaign Strategy

The proposed campaign uses the Leads objective and three ad sets for audience testing.

Performance is evaluated using:

- Reach
- Impressions
- Click-Through Rate (CTR)
- Cost Per Click (CPC)
- Cost Per Lead (CPL)

The strategy also includes budget optimization, creative comparison, Custom Audiences, Lookalike Audiences, and retargeting.
